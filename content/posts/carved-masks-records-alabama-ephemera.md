This round brought in a mix of carved wooden pieces, records, and small paper finds. A few carved wooden masks stand out, including a bearded face with dark carved hair and a couple of smaller, more angular masks in a lighter wood.

There's a stack of vinyl records in the pile, with a Sugarhill Gang greatest hits album sitting on top, along with a B.B. King in London record and a few others mixed in. Paper items include a felt Alabama State University pennant, a Talladega College basketball program, a paperback called "Hill of Beans," and a couple of arts festival programs and sketches.

Rounding things out are some smaller everyday items — a leather wallet, a small carved wooden box, a set of flatware, and a canvas bag the pieces were photographed on top of.

We'll be sorting through all of it over the next few weeks, so keep an eye out as pieces get listed across our shops.
