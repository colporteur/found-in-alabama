This one came from a two-day estate sale on Fairway Drive in Jacksonville, Alabama, held out of a stone-and-shingle house tucked back under a lot of trees. The listing just called it a complete home full of unusual pieces, and after loading up the car, that tracks.

The haul leans heavily toward paper. There are hardback hymnals and religious education books, a copy of Gospel Parallels, and a stack of loose tracts and pamphlets. Mixed in with those are clipped comic pages, some old magazines, and a set of papers and clippings related to the moon landing. One of the more eye-catching pieces is a rolled panorama print of Jerusalem, long enough that it stands on end.

We are still working through boxes from this one, so there is more to sort and photograph. Check back as pieces from the Jacksonville haul start coming online across our shops.
