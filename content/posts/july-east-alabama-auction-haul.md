We spent July 18 at an East Alabama auction and came home with a yellow shopping cart full of odds and ends, plus several boxes more sitting alongside it.

There's a stack of hardback books topped with what looks like a ledger or scrapbook, a woven tapestry-style throw, and a wire basket of what appear to be knit or crocheted pieces. Vinyl records are stacked in a couple of boxes, including one with a visible Toto album cover. Kitchen items include a stainless electric skillet with its lid on and a Keurig coffee maker. There are boxes of CDs, a stack of automotive repair manuals including a Chevrolet full-size models guide, a green ceramic jug, and a cardboard box holding what looks like framed pieces or trays along with a couple of mugs.

The yellow cart itself is loaded with tool bags, including one marked Ryobi, along with some cords and small hardware.

It's a wide mix this time — books, music, kitchen gear, tools, and a little bit of everything else. We'll be sorting through it all and listing pieces as we go, so keep an eye out as items come online.
