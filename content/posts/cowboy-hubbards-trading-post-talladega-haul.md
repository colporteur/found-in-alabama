This haul came from a private pick of the remaining inventory of the old Cowboy Hubbard's Farmers Trading Post in Talladega, Alabama. It was inherited by a relative of the late Cowboy Hubbard, and some other family collections came along with it. There may be a chance to bring in more down the road.

Most of it is paper. There's a good bit of Alabama ephemera, including a printed thank-you ad on the back of a Farmer's Almanac from "Cowboy" Hubbard's Farmers Trading Post and Mary Carter Paint Store in Talladega, and an advertising thermometer from the Talladega County Exchange. There are also a few old advertising calendars, one showing January 1958, along with an embossed wall piece with a cottage, a deer, and flowers across the top. A 1992 Grier's Almanac is in the mix too.

On the newspaper side, there are copies of the Marietta Daily Journal, including one with the headline "Atlanta bids farewell to Olympics" and others covering the Braves. For periodicals, there's a stack of LIFE magazines, a Railroad Magazine, and a newsprint section titled "The Sixties" with an astronaut on the cover. Rounding things out are some sheet music, including "You Were Only Fooling (While I Was Falling in Love)," a few programs, and a metal file box full of index cards, which were paint records of customers with colors, dates painted, etc from the store in Talladega.

We'll be sorting through all of it and listing pieces as we go. Keep an eye on the shop as items come online.
