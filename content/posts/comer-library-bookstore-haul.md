Spent an afternoon at the B. B. Comer Memorial Library's bookstore, where they had a buy-one-get-one free sale going. Came home with a pretty full box.

The bulk of it is a lot of southern gospel LPs, including one from the McKeithens called "Silver & Gold." There's a stack of CDs too, among them a Tommy Sellers disc called "Mississippi Lady," and a few audiobooks on CD, including Wanda E. Brunstetter's "Sarah's Choice."

On the book side, there's a biography of Janie Shores titled "Trailblazing Supreme Court Justice," a Kentucky D.A.R. cookbook, a hardback copy of Kahlil Gibran's "The Voice of the Master," and Robert Wuthnow's "The Restructuring of American Religion." Also picked up a 16-volume set of Mircea Eliade's Encyclopedia of Religion, which isn't pictured here but came home in the same trip.

Nothing fancy, just a good mix from a library sale. Keep an eye out as these make their way online across our usual spots.
