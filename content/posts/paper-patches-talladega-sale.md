This batch came from a three-day estate sale on Roll Tide Drive in Talladega, the kind of listing that promised a packed three-car garage along with tools, Harley-Davidson items, and a mix of vintage collectibles. What came home with us leaned toward the paper and small-collectibles side of that mix.

There's a stack of old photo portraits in cardboard frames, a tote full of loose papers and ephemera, and a run of yearbooks alongside a stack of Bibles. Spread across the table are vintage Alabama license plates, a handful of cloth patches including sheriff's department and police patches from several counties, and a couple of old bank bags from local banks.

A few named pieces stand out: a "Hank Williams' Country Hit Parade" songbook, a Peter Rabbit storybook, a "The Good Humor Man" title, and a copy of "The Wonderful World of" magazine or book. There's also a small ceramic Alabama-themed pitcher, some old sports newspaper pages with an Alabama football theme, and a coffee can holding loose buttons and small hardware.

We'll be sorting through all of it over the next few weeks. Keep an eye on our listings as these pieces come online one by one.
