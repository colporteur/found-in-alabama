From another Anniston, Alabama estate sale:

This week's finds lean toward paper. There's an exhibition album for Nicholas & Alexandra with a red cover, a Cobb County, Georgia booklet with a columned home on the front, and a couple of wooden-handled advertising hand fans, one for Jacksonville State University and another marking the 125th anniversary of First Baptist Church of Weaver, AL. A third fan from Jones-Wynn Funeral Home rounds those out, along with a small plaque bearing a gold shield emblem.

There's also a good handful of postcards in the mix, showing places like Lake McDonald Lodge, Many Glacier Hotel, and Piazza Navona, plus a Musei Vaticani ticket-style card. A separate postcard set shows close-up photos of ornate objects, including a dagger and small vessels.

All of it looks like it was kept together as a small paper collection rather than sorted by subject. We'll be photographing and listing pieces individually over the coming days, so check back as they come online across our shops.
